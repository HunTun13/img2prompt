Check what the concept image changed

  • Geometry: confirm that windows, doors, walls and ceiling stayed where the reference showed them.
  • Scale: treat furniture size in generated images as approximate until you have real measurements.
  • Light: note whether the new fixtures and daylight direction make sense for the room.

Interior design prompts FAQ

Can the prompt preserve my exact room layout?

It can describe visible room geometry and tell an image model what to keep, but generated concepts can still move walls, doors or windows. Inspect each result against the reference.

Should I upload an empty room or a furnished one?

Either works. An empty room makes architecture easier to see; a furnished room gives useful clues about scale, lighting and the current style.

Can I use a generated image as a renovation plan?

No. It is a visual concept. Confirm dimensions, structure, electrical work, materials and safety requirements with qualified professionals before building.
